# HEOS Constitution

## The Constitution of Home Energy Operating System

### Article I — Explainability

Every decision made by HEOS must be explainable.

No hidden decisions.

No black boxes.

---

### Article II — Safety

Safety always overrides optimization.

Comfort always overrides savings.

---

### Article III — Local First

The intelligence belongs to the home.

Cloud services are optional.

---

### Article IV — Vendor Neutrality

No manufacturer shall receive special treatment.

All vendors are equal.

---

### Article V — Modularity

Everything is replaceable.

Nothing is hardcoded.

---

### Article VI — Transparency

Every Brain must publish:

- Inputs
- Outputs
- Reasoning
- Confidence

---

### Article VII — Human Override

The owner of the house always has the final decision.

HEOS advises.

Humans decide.

---

### Article VIII — Continuous Learning

HEOS evolves.

Knowledge never stops growing.
